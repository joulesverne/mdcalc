Math lines compute. Anything that doesn't parse is prose.

x = 12          assign a variable; its value shows in grey
x * 4           any line that parses shows its value
_ + 1           _  is the previous successful result
# notes         comments: a whole line, or the end of one

+  -  *  /      arithmetic           9 / 2       = 4.5
//              floor division       9 // 2      = 4
%               modulo               9 % 2       = 1
**              exponent (not ^)     2 ** 10     = 1024
( )             grouping             (1 + 2) * 3 = 9

sqrt(x)         square root          sqrt(81)    = 9
exp(x)          e to the power x     exp(1)      = 2.71828
log(x)          natural log (ln)     log(e)      = 1
log(x, b)       log, base b          log(8, 2)   = 3
log2 log10      fixed-base logs      log10(1000) = 3
sin cos tan     radians              cos(pi)     = -1
asin acos atan  inverses, radians    atan(1)     = 0.785398
floor ceil      round down / up      ceil(2.1)   = 3
abs(x)          absolute value       abs(-7)     = 7
round(x)        nearest, ties even   round(2.5)  = 2
round(x, d)     d decimal places     round(pi, 2)= 3.14
factorial(n)                         factorial(5)= 120
min max         any number of args   max(4, 9, 2)= 9

pi  e  tau      constants            pi          = 3.14159

1/0, sqrt(-1), and overflow show "error"; so does an
assignment whose right side is broken (total = pricee * 2).
